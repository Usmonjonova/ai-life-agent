# Problem and Users

## Primary user

A motivated person who has meaningful long-term goals but struggles with consistency, prioritization, planning realism, or understanding why plans repeatedly fail.

## Initial validation user

The product builder herself.

This is intentional: the first version should solve a real problem for one person rather than attempting to solve every productivity problem for everyone.

## User jobs-to-be-done

### Before planning

- Help me clarify what I actually want.
- Help me distinguish important goals from vague wishes.
- Help me decide what deserves attention now.

### During planning

- Turn a goal into milestones.
- Turn milestones into realistic weekly actions.
- Prevent me from overplanning.
- Consider my available time and constraints.

### During execution

- Remind me at useful moments.
- Help me start difficult tasks.
- Let me quickly report completion, delay, or failure.
- Reduce friction when plans change.

### After execution

- Help me understand what happened.
- Identify patterns behind missed tasks.
- Distinguish a bad plan from a bad execution day.
- Adapt the next plan based on evidence.

## Key pain points

1. Too many goals at once.
2. Plans that ignore real constraints.
3. Large tasks that are difficult to start.
4. Forgetting priorities during the day.
5. Excessive entertainment/distraction.
6. Repeatedly postponing the same task.
7. Feeling guilty without learning from the failure.
8. Lack of a coherent long-term picture.

## Product insight

A missed task is not automatically a discipline problem.

It can indicate:

- poor estimation;
- low energy;
- competing commitments;
- unclear task definition;
- insufficient motivation;
- external interruption;
- task anxiety;
- an unrealistic plan.

The agent should investigate patterns rather than immediately blame the user.
